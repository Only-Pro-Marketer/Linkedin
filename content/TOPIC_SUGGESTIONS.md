## KEY PATTERNS — What Works vs What Doesn't

> This section populates after the system has enough data to identify patterns.
> Generally, look for:
>
> **What tends to work on LinkedIn:**
> - Posts with specific numbers and real examples
> - Personal / behind-the-scenes content
> - Case studies with concrete results
> - Contrarian takes backed by evidence
> - Text-only format (often outperforms image posts)
> - Weekday mornings
>
> **What tends to underperform:**
> - Generic advice without specifics
> - Listicles without story context
> - Weekend and evening posts
> - Holiday greeting posts

## CONTENT CALENDAR RECOMMENDATIONS

### Posting Schedule (Customize Based on Your Data)

- **Best days:** Test and track — typically weekdays outperform weekends

### Weekly Mix (Example)

- **Thursday:** Personal story or behind-the-scenes
- **Friday:** Quick tactical tip or trend commentary
